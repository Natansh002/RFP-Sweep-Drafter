# Assignments

This folder is how people assign and update findings on the published site.

1. Open the site, pick your company, click **Download Excel**.
2. Edit the yellow ✎ columns: Status, Assignee (a role), Notes, Est. value, Loss
   reason, Awardee; action items (add rows to add actions); compliance owner and status.
3. Save it here as `<company-id>.xlsx`, for example `acme.xlsx`,
   and commit it (GitHub's **Add file → Upload files** works).
4. The site rebuilds and shows your changes. The committed sheet is authoritative for
   those columns on every build.

This repository and its site are public. Use role titles, not people's names, and keep
notes free of customer data, pricing and anything confidential.
